Step-by-Step Syllabus

6 Core Tajweed Milestones

Drag horizontally or let the roadmap glide through the core pillars of your recitation journey.

Milestone 01

Makharij & Sifaat

Pinpoint the 17 articulation points from throat to lips and apply heavy/light letter qualities.

Milestone 02

Noon & Meem Sakinah

Master Izhar, Idgham, Iqlab, Ikhfa, and nasal Ghunnah timing with clarity.

Milestone 03

Rules of Madd

Measure natural and secondary elongations accurately (2, 4, 5, and 6 harakat).

Milestone 04

Qalqalah & Tafkheem

Apply crisp echoing letters and proper heaviness/lightness (Tafkheem & Tarqeeq) on Ra & Lam.

Milestone 05

Rules of Waqf

Learn where and how to pause and resume breath without altering Quranic meaning.

Milestone 06

Juz Amma Fluency

Apply all Tajweed rules fluidly across Juz Amma and continue smoothly into full Quran recitation.
